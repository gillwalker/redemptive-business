Before you begin

  • Please complete this form at least 7 days before your group meeting so the other members have time to review it. Gill will send your responses to the group.
  • Please do not read your presentation to the group. Assume they have read what you have prepared. As you open, simply ask if there are any questions, comments, thoughts, or observations your group members have – let this be a conversation, and allow them to probe accordingly.
  • You may also bring literature or other information on your company, or display your company website on the conference room screen.
  • You have up to 45 minutes to share your presentation to the group. Remember to get to your issue(s) as soon as possible to gain the most value.

Your answers save automatically in this browser as you go, so you can close the page and come back later on the same device. Nothing is sent until you press Submit on the last page.
