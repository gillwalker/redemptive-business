The film
Adam Smith: The Currency of Thought
A short documentary · ~25 minutes · Director & producer Jon Cipiti will be with us

Never heard of Adam Smith? You've lived in his world. The 18th-century Scottish philosopher is called the “Father of Capitalism,” and his ideas about free markets and the “invisible hand” still shape how nearly every business operates today.

Those who do know him usually know only The Wealth of Nations. But Smith wrote another book first, The Theory of Moral Sentiments, and he believed it was the place to start.

This film introduces the whole Smith: a thinker who cared about sympathy, character, and the good of our neighbors as much as markets and self-interest.

Think again.

Why it matters

What should capitalism look like for a follower of Jesus?

If you lead a business, you live inside this question every day: in how you price, hire, compete, and define success.

Many of us have inherited a thin version of capitalism, one that treats profit as the only goal and people as a means to it. We'll use the film to ask a better question: what does it look like to run a business that is both profitable and redemptive, one that serves customers, employees, and community and honors God in the process?

Who this is for

Owners and founders of small and medium-sized businesses who follow Christ and feel the tension of honoring God while growing a healthy, profitable company, often without many peers who understand. If you own or run a business, this morning is for you.

What this isn't

It's not a networking event (though we hope you'll meet some great people), and it's not a place for sales or marketing. It's an honest conversation about what God says about your work. It's good news.
